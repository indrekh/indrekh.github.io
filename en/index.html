          Why check your certificate regularly?
          
        
          
          
          
            
              
            
            
          Visitor trust
          
            
          Browsers show a warning when opening a site with an expired certificate, and visitors leave immediately – taking their trust with them.
          
          
          
          
          
            
              
            
            
          Search engines
          
            
          HTTPS is a Google ranking factor. A broken certificate drops your site well down the search results, or out of them entirely.
          
          
          
          
          
            
              
            
            
          Encrypted traffic
          
            
          SSL/TLS encrypts the connection between visitor and server. An expired certificate leaves the data open to interception and tampering.
          
          
          
        
          
      
          
    
          

    
          
      
          
        
          Renewing a certificate and understanding the result
          
        
          
          
          
            
          What the result means
          
            
          
              
          Issuer
          The certificate authority (CA) that issued and digitally signed the certificate – for example Let's Encrypt or Google Trust Services.
          
              
          Validity
          A certificate is valid only during the stated period. After the end date the browser displays a warning page.
          
              
          Fingerprint
          A unique SHA-256 identifier that lets you verify the server is serving exactly this certificate.
          
              
          Covered hostnames
          All addresses the certificate covers (SAN). If your address is not listed, the browser will show a warning.
          
            
          
          
          
          
          
            
          How to renew a certificate
          
            
          In most cases a free Let's Encrypt certificate and Certbot are enough. Renew at least 30 days before expiry.
          
            
            
              
          1. Install Certbot: sudo apt install certbot python3-certbot-nginx (Nginx) or python3-certbot-apache (Apache).
            2. 
              
          2. Obtain and install the certificate: sudo certbot --nginx -d example.com (or --apache).
            3. 
              
          3. Test automatic renewal: sudo certbot renew --dry-run. Certbot sets up the scheduled task for you.
            4. 
            
          
            
          Tip: if you use Cloudflare, you can manage SSL from its dashboard instead – no Certbot needed on the server.
          
          
          
        
          
      
          
    
          

    
          
      
          
        
          Frequently asked questions
          
        
          
          
          
            What does this site do?
            
          ssl.ee checks a domain's SSL/TLS certificate validity: how long it is valid, who issued it, and which hostnames it protects. Just type a domain name – you get an answer within seconds.
          
          
          
          
          
            Where does the data come from?
            
          Certificate data is read from Certificate Transparency logs via Cert Spotter (with crt.sh as backup); the HTTP security-header score comes from MDN HTTP Observatory, the full chain and CT/chain comparison from Trustico SSL Tools, and CAA/DNSSEC from Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS. All requests run directly in your browser.
          
          
          
          
          
            Why might the information differ from reality?
            
          We show the most recently issued certificate from the CT logs. A server or CDN may sometimes serve a different (e.g. older) certificate – for a full analysis (protocols and ciphers included) use the SSL Labs link below.
          
          
          
          
          
            What does "expiring soon" mean?
            
          Less than 30 days remain on the certificate. We recommend renewing right away so the site doesn't go dark because a renewal was forgotten.
          
          
          
          
          
            Is it free to use?
            
          Yes. ssl.ee is free, requires no sign-up and collects no personal data. The upstream data sources may apply their own rate limits.
